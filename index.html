02 · How we build

Rules the software follows, every time

They come from years of working on failed drives in a recovery lab, where the wrong click costs someone their data.

O_RDONLY

Read, never write

The drive being recovered is opened read-only, and recovered files can only be saved to a different drive.

SMART 05 · C5 · C6

Health before scanning

The drive's own health report is read first. A drive that reports failure isn't scanned, and one showing warning signs is imaged once instead of read over and over.

EXIT 0

Know when to stop

When a drive clicks, isn't detected or shows 0 MB, software is the wrong tool. Ours says so and points to a lab.

Why XOR

Exclusive OR returns 1 when its two inputs differ. It is also its own inverse: apply the same value twice and you are back where you started.

That property is what lets a RAID 5 array survive a dead disk. The parity block is every data block XORed together, so any one missing block equals the XOR of everything that is left. The panel at the top of this page does exactly that, one byte at a time.

Recovery is the same idea at every scale: find what is still there, and work out what is missing from it.
